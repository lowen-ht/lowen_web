# 学习资源

## 权威书籍

- Benjamin Graham《聪明的投资者》(The Intelligent Investor) — 价值投资基础
- Burton Malkiel《漫步华尔街》(A Random Walk Down Wall Street) — 有效市场与[[investment-knowledge-base#指数|指数]]投资
- Philip Fisher《Common Stocks and Uncommon Profits》— 成长股定性分析
- Peter Lynch《One Up on Wall Street》《Beating the Street》— 散户视角的选股
- Morgan Housel《金钱心理学》(The Psychology of Money) — 行为金融与心态
- Ray Dalio《原则》《Big Debt Crises》— 全天候配置与宏观
- Jeremy Siegel《Stocks for the [[investment-knowledge-base#多头与仓位|Long]] Run》— 长期股权回报证据
- David Swensen《Pioneering Portfolio Management》— 机构配置思想
